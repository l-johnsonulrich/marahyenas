What Your Donation Funds

Field Operations

Daily vehicle runs, fuel, equipment maintenance, and camp infrastructure for two field camps running continuously in the Maasai Mara.

Kenyan Staff

Salaries and benefits for our dedicated Kenyan field team, many of whom have worked with the MHP for over a decade and are essential to data collection.

Community Outreach

Hyena education programs for local schools, free conflict resolution consulting for farmers, and training workshops for Kenyan students and researchers.
