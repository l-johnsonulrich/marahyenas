For Researchers

Data Access & Collaboration

The MHP maintains one of the most extensive longitudinal behavioral datasets on any wild carnivore population. Researchers interested in collaborative data analysis or field research partnerships should contact the relevant Co-Director directly. See our current projects for active research areas.

For data sharing inquiries: marahyenaproject@gmail.com

The Mara Hyena Project has monitored spotted hyena clans in Kenya’s Maasai Mara since 1988.
