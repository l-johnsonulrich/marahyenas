Our Team

The Mara Hyena Project is supported by the passion and dedication of an incredible team of people from diverse backgrounds across two continents.

Leadership

Co-Directors

Dr. Kay Holekamp

Founder & Co-Director

Dr. Holekamp founded the MHP in 1988. She is a University Distinguished Professor at Michigan State University whose research focuses on mammalian behavioral development, social behavior, and the evolution of intelligence. Co-author of over 200 peer-reviewed publications.

holekamplab.org ↗
ZL

Dr. Zach Laubach

Co-Director

Dr. Laubach studies how early-life social environments influence gene expression, stress physiology, and long-term fitness outcomes in hyenas, bridging behavioral ecology and molecular biology.

laubach.github.io ↗

Dr. Eli Strauss

Co-Director

Dr. Strauss is a behavioral ecologist at the Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior. He investigates how stable social systems emerge from diverse individuals, using field experiments, computational methods, and long-term data.

straussed.github.io ↗
KL

Dr. Kenna Lehmann

Co-Director

Dr. Lehmann’s research investigates the mechanisms and consequences of social behavior in spotted hyenas, with a focus on how sociality shapes individual fitness.

Website ↗
TM

Dr. Tracy Montgomery

Co-Director

Dr. Montgomery studies behavioral variation and its ecological consequences in spotted hyenas, examining how individual differences in behavior influence survival and reproduction.

Website ↗

Dr. Lily Johnson-Ulrich

Co-Director

Dr. Johnson-Ulrich is an Assistant Professor at Hunter College (CUNY). Her research focuses on the evolution of cognitive traits and how urbanization acts as a natural experiment to investigate cognitive ecology and behavioral adaptation.

lilyjohnsonulrich.weebly.com ↗
